Why drinking matters

Water is the basis of almost everything that happens in your body. Still, drinking easily gets lost in daily life. A drinking tracker shows how much you really drink and helps you keep going.

50–60 %Share of water in an adult's body
1.5 lDGE guideline for drinks per day under normal conditions
2.0–2.5 lTotal water per day from drinks and food (EFSA, women and men)

1 · The problem

Between appointments, work and errands, drinking gets lost. Often you only notice when the day is almost over.

2 · What happens

Thirst often shows up late. Too little fluid can show, for example, as tiredness or reduced concentration.

3 · The solution

Daily Hydration keeps your goal in view: one tap per drink, a fill gauge and reminders that gently get you to drink.

What drinking can do for you

Concentration

Water contributes to the maintenance of normal cognitive function. If you drink throughout the day, it is easier to stay focused.

How the app helps: Reminders gently bring you back to your glass.

Exercise and sport

During sport and physical work you lose fluid through sweat. Your needs go up, and it is worth keeping an eye on the amount.

How the app helps: The automatic daily goal takes your daily activity into account.

Heat and body temperature

Water contributes to the maintenance of normal body temperature. On hot days you usually need more than usual.

How the app helps: If you like, the current temperature feeds into your daily goal.

Digestion

Together with a diet rich in fibre, enough fluid supports normal digestion.

How the app helps: The history shows whether you drink enough regularly across the week.

Body functions

Water is the solvent and transport medium for nutrients and metabolic products. Without enough fluid, a lot does not run smoothly.

How the app helps: The fill gauge shows you right away how far along you are today.

Habit

Small routines work better than good intentions. If you log every glass, you drink more mindfully.

How the app helps: One tap per drink, plus widgets on your home screen.

These notes are general guidance and do not replace medical advice. Your personal needs depend on age, weight, activity, temperature and health, among other things. With certain conditions, for example of the heart or kidneys, other amounts may apply. Talk to your doctor in that case.

Sources: German Nutrition Society (DGE), drinking recommendations; EFSA, reference values for water and authorised health claims on water.

Good to know

Answers to questions we often get.

How much should I drink per day?

As a guideline, the DGE names about 1.5 litres of drinks per day for adults under normal conditions. Your personal needs depend on age, weight, activity, temperature and health, among other things. That is why the app can also calculate your daily goal automatically.

Do coffee and tea count?

In usual amounts, coffee and tea also contribute to your fluid intake. In the app you log them like water, and every logged drink counts towards your daily amount. The analysis shows you the share of each drink type.

How do I notice that I drink too little?

Possible signs are thirst, tiredness, reduced concentration, a dry mouth or dark urine. These are only indications. With strong or lasting complaints, talk to your doctor.

When do I need more?

In heat, during sport and physical work your body loses more fluid, and so it does with fever or diarrhoea. Needs can also be higher during pregnancy and breastfeeding. With conditions such as of the heart or kidneys, other amounts may apply. Ask your doctor in that case.

Does the app cost anything?

The core features are free, with no account and no ads. Some container designs and extended analysis are part of Premium.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. On the iPhone you can sync it with your other devices through your private iCloud account. We do not run our own server and have no access to your data.
